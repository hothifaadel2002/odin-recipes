Pizza

Description

Homemade Margherita Pizza is a timeless Italian favorite featuring a crispy, golden-brown crust topped with rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil leaves. Simple yet bursting with flavor, it is easy to make from scratch and perfect for sharing..

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup pizza tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves and a pinch of oregano for topping

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, warm water, and olive oil in a bowl, then knead for 5 minutes until a smooth dough forms.
  2. Cover the dough and let it rise in a warm place for about 30 to 45 minutes until doubled in size.
  3. Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F), roll out the dough into a round base, and place it on a baking tray.
  4. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough, top with mozzarella cheese and oregano, and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  5. Garnish with fresh basil leaves, slice, and serve hot.
