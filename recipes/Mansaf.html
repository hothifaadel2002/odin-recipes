Mansaf

Description

Mansaf is Jordan's traditional national dish, featuring tender lamb cooked in a rich, tangy sauce made from fermented dried yogurt called Jameed. Served over a generous layer of aromatic turmeric rice and thin flatbread (Shrak), and topped with toasted almonds and pine nuts, it is a celebratory meal known for its deep, authentic flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg bone-in lamb chunks
  • 2 balls of Jameed (fermented dried yogurt), soaked and blended
  • 2 cups medium-grain or basmati rice
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 sheets of thin flatbread (Shrak)
  • 1/2 cup blanched almonds and pine nuts
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or vegetable oil
  • Fresh chopped parsley for garnish
  • Whole spices (cardamom pods, bay leaves, cinnamon stick) and salt

Steps

  1. Soak the Jameed in warm water for a few hours, then blend it until smooth to create a creamy yogurt liquid.
  2. Boil the lamb with whole spices until tender, skimming off any foam, then pour the blended Jameed into the pot and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Cook the rice with ghee, water, salt, and a pinch of turmeric until fluffy and golden yellow.
  4. Toast the almonds and pine nuts in a small pan with ghee until golden brown.
  5. Lay the Shrak bread on a large serving platter, layer the yellow rice on top, arrange the tender lamb pieces, garnish with nuts and parsley, and serve with warm Jameed sauce on the side.
